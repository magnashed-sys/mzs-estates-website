# Operations completion release — 28 October 2026

Authorized production scope: internal maintenance work orders and budget decisions; private ticket photos; a local accountant export by issuer/year; concept cost reporting by month/property/category. Manual PDF intake and evidence-based purchase approval remain deferred.

## Behavior

- Admin opens a maintenance ticket in `/admin/workspaces/estates/staff`. A triaged ticket and an explicitly verified, currently effective payer link are required for a work-order draft. Supplier, quotation reference, scope and separate net/VAT budget amounts are recorded. Admin can approve or decline a draft and subsequently cancel an internal approval. Immutable decision history and expected preceding decision IDs reject stale submissions. No supplier message, external instruction, invoice or payment is generated.
- The ticket detail accepts up to three private photographs from its reporter with current property access, or an active administrator. Input is at most 4 MiB, a single JPEG/PNG/WebP image and at most 20 million pixels. Sharp decodes and creates an oriented JPEG bounded to 2560 pixels; EXIF/GPS and appended data are not copied. Downloads recheck current RLS, registered hash/size and decode again; no signed URL or public bucket. This is image normalization, not an antivirus claim. An admin can permanently hide a photograph in the application with an audited reason. Registered files cannot be replaced/deleted by users; no automatic retention/deletion schedule is asserted. Failed registrations attempt removal of the uploader's unregistered object. An interrupted cleanup can require operator cleanup through Storage APIs; never delete storage metadata directly.
- `/admin/workspaces/estates/reports` displays **concept** costs by original invoice month, property and category, with issuer/year/property filters. These are neither approved expenses nor payments and do not change the existing cashflow calculation. Evidence approval waits for the future document-ingestion workflow.
- The same page downloads an issuer/year ZIP containing issued rental/booking invoices, dated receipt events, administrative reversals, undated exceptions, README and SHA-256 manifest. One security-invoker SQL statement supplies a consistent source snapshot. Rental net/VAT remains unknown, undated receipts have no invented year, and reversals do not imply bank refunds. Uninvoiced bookings, unattributable payments, purchase concepts and Capital remain outside this partial export. Ambiguous/oversized/incomplete sources fail closed; output is bounded below the hosting response limit.
