# Optional company billing and correspondence details

The central company register at `/admin/relationships?kind=company` supports optional trading name, registration/KvK number, VAT/tax number, contact person/attention and HTTPS website. The existing address remains the default address and keeps its stored values. Invoice and correspondence sections each provide a separate attention line, email and structured address. Each empty override falls back to its general company value. Addresses are selected as whole blocks, never merged field by field. Clearing an override restores the fallback. All new fields may be left empty.

Selecting a company in a contract draft copies its effective invoice address/email, attention and VAT number into editable contract fields. Saving freezes these values in that contract; invoice preparation copies the contract snapshot, and invoice previews and PDFs display the attention and VAT number when present. Changes to a company do not rewrite any saved contract or invoice. Correspondence details are retained in the central company record for correspondence workflows; they do not route invoice emails. No mail is sent by saving company data.

Migration `20261025133509_company_billing_details.sql` adds a bounded `company_details` JSON object to the admin-only relationship table and optional `tenant_tax_id`/`tenant_attention` columns to contracts. No existing columns, rows, permissions or historical snapshots are rewritten. Active-contract locking already compares all columns. Older forms omit the new fields on save, preserving their values. Existing optimistic version checks protect concurrent edits. This does not change member-declared company affiliations under My profile.

Validation: automated model and real PostgreSQL-compatible database tests cover empty fields, length/type/email/address validation, independent fallbacks, clearing, RLS, migration preservation, active-contract locks and immutable invoice copies. A local browser fixture covers create, save, reload, edit, clear and copy into contracts, plus the existing issuer → contract → invoice → captured email flow on desktop and mobile widths. The fixture uses synthetic in-memory data and captured email. Native Safari and signed-in Production acceptance are not automated.
